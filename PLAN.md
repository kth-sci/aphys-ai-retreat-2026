## Design principles

Carried over from the initiative as a whole:

- **Agent-first** — agents are the interface for learning and doing, not lectures
- **Physics-grounded** — every example uses Applied Physics contexts (spectroscopy, microscopy, materials, time-resolved measurements)
- **Personalised** — the AI Tutor Prompt adapts to each participant's research area and level
- **Action-oriented** — every session produces something concrete
- **Critical** — capabilities and limitations shown together; domain knowledge beats black-box tools
