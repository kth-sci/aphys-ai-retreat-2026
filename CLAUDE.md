# APHYS AI Retreat 2026 — Project Instructions

## What this is

Planning repository for the **Applied Physics Department AI Retreat**, October
21–22, 2026, at KTH Royal Institute of Technology. Two full days, ~25–30 faculty
from the Department of Applied Physics, led by **Wei Ouyang** and **Jonas
Sellberg**.

This is the fourth event in the 2026 APHYS AI series and the deep technical one.
The three before it — the May 8 tutorial, the May 26 SCI hackathon, and the June
teachers' meeting — built basic agent familiarity. This retreat assumes it.

The initiative hub lives in

reusing it.

## Design philosophy

- **Agent-first** — agents are the interface for learning and doing, not lectures
- **Physics-grounded** — examples come from Applied Physics: spectroscopy, microscopy, materials, time-resolved measurements
- **Personalised** — content adapts to each participant's research area and level
- **Action-oriented** — every session produces a concrete output
- **Critical** — show capabilities and limitations together; domain knowledge beats black-box tools

## Audience

Senior academics, not students. They have strong quantitative backgrounds and
little patience for hype — the gap is practical tool usage, not theory. Cite by
name and year, and only claims that survive push-back.
